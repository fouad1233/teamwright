# 0003. License under Apache-2.0

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-11

## Context

Teamwright is meant to be used widely, to accept contributions, and to be
something companies can adopt without a legal review that takes longer than
the evaluation.

## Decision

The project is licensed under the Apache License 2.0.

## Consequences

- Anyone may use, change and redistribute Teamwright, including commercially.
- Contributors grant a patent license for their contributions.
- The license does not grant rights to the project's name or logo.
- Dependencies must have licenses compatible with Apache-2.0. Copyleft
  dependencies need a decision record of their own.
- Artwork created for the project ships under the same license.

## Alternatives considered

- **MIT.** Shorter and equally permissive, without the patent grant and the
  explicit trademark clause.
- **AGPL-3.0.** Would keep modified versions open, at the price of excluding
  some companies and contributors.
