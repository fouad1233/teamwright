# The Teamwright story

This folder is the project's chronicle: what happened, when, and why. It has
two readers.

- **Contributors** who want to know how the project came to be the way it is.
- **The film.** The plan is to turn this history into a short animated
  explainer. Every chapter therefore ends with *storyboard beats*: concrete
  moments that can be drawn.

## How chapters are written

- One chapter per milestone or working session, numbered in order.
- Past tense, plain words, real dates and real numbers.
- Four sections: **What happened**, **Why**, **Decisions**, **Storyboard
  beats**.
- A decision with lasting technical weight also gets a record in
  [`docs/decisions`](../decisions/README.md), and the chapter links to it.
- Chapters are not rewritten to look wiser in hindsight. Corrections are
  added as dated notes at the end of the chapter.
- Failures and wrong assumptions belong in the story. They are usually the
  best scenes.
