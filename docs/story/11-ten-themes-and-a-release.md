# Chapter 11: Ten themes and a release

*17 and 18 November 2026*

## What happened

Before going to sleep on 17 November the founder asked for three things
and added two more while the first was being built. A choice of theme in
the settings, with palettes "scientifically good for human eyes and
productivity" and "special" ones with patterns, "maybe flowers or birds",
the present look staying the default. The advertisement brought up to
date with every view, every integration and all ten themes, in a dark and
a light version to choose from. Then: the logos of the products
Teamwright works with, an integration with Google Drive, and a release
with installers for Windows, macOS and Linux.

**The themes.** Ten, kept for one computer. Lamplight stays the default.
Paper, Dusk, Slate, Clear and Even are built for the eyes, and each says
plainly what it does: no palette claims to make anybody productive,
because nothing shows that a colour does. Their contrast ratios are
tested. Blossom, Wing, Fern and Tide carry a pattern drawn in code, plum
blossoms, swallows, fronds and waves. The window crosses from one theme
to the next in four tenths of a second.

**Google Drive.** The founder asked how the large trackers do it. They
register once with Google, and every user signs in to Google through
them; that registration is the founder's to make and was left for later.
What was built works today: Teamwright finds the folder that Google's own
program keeps on the computer and syncs through it in one press, with no
sign-in and no token. This computer has no such program, so the run in
the built program went against a pretended folder.

**The logos.** The marks of GitHub and Google Drive came from the
companies' own brand pages and are kept unaltered, with a note of where
they came from and the rules that bind their use. Slack gives its mark
only to people signed in to its media kit, so Slack stays a word.

**The release.** A workflow builds the installers when a version is
tagged. Version 0.1.0 went out on 18 November as a pre-release, for
Windows, both kinds of Mac and Linux. The Mac build had never been tried
before and went through at the first attempt. Nothing is signed, and the
notes of the release say what the systems show because of that.

**The film.** A second film was made beside the first: the new part
names the views, the integrations, the sync, the notifications, the
workbook and the themes, and ends with one board crossing through all ten
themes without a cut. It is 165 seconds, 58 more than the first, in a
dark and a light version. The website keeps the first film until the
founder has chosen.

## Why it went this way

**Honest about the eyes.** "Scientifically good" was taken at its word:
what is known is said (contrast, warm and dim in the evening, no pure
white on black, status told apart without colour vision) and nothing
more.

**No token for Google either.** The same rule as for the specialists and
for GitHub: through the manager's own program, or not yet.
